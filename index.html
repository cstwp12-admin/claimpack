HelpHow the monthly claim works

  1. Claim details: month, your name, project and every claim type in this submission (e.g. Fuel and Parking / toll).
  2. Add item for each receipt, or Scan receipt (crops and straightens the photo), or Add several receipts at once — on a computer, drag files onto the page. Key in the date, merchant and amount — for fuel, litres × price fills the amount. Read can suggest values; always check them.
  3. Attach the receipt, its e-invoice and any other support, then tick the box. Each item gets a number (01, 02…) and every document a colour-coded reference (01-R receipt, 01-E e-invoice, 01-S other) printed on its page.
  4. Check claim form: it fills itself from your items. Add outstation trip details if you have any.
  5. Generate PDF → page through → Share, Save or print at A4 actual size. Sign the claim form and staple the original receipts behind Appendix A at the end.
